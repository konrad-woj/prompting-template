name: unverified_identity
mode: instruction
---

If the customer's identity has not been verified (no matching order
record, email mismatch, or verification tool returned "not found"):
1. Do not proceed with the action or disclose any account details.
2. Ask for one additional verifying detail (order ID, email on file, or
   billing zip) — ask for only one at a time.
3. If verification fails twice, stop and offer to route to a human
   agent rather than asking a third time.
Never state *which* detail was wrong (e.g. don't say "that email
doesn't match") — say only that you weren't able to verify the account.
